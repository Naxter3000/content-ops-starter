📖 How to Play
Throwing
  • Move the mouse to aim
  • Hold the mouse button to charge power
  • Release to throw
Curl
  • Stones curve as they slow down
  • While charging, move mouse left or right from where you clicked to set curl
  • The curl bar shows direction & amount
Scoring
  • Only the team with the closest stone scores
  • They score 1 point per stone closer than any opponent stone
  • Stones must be inside the house (outer ring)
Hammer
  • The scoring team throws first next end
  • Going last is an advantage — called the hammer
  • First end decided by coin flip
Game
  • 4 stones each per end
  • 3 ends total
  • Highest score wins!
